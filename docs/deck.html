ProofLine

Verified economic history becomes reusable credit.

Ethereum contains evidence of economic behaviour. Attestcoin lets Creditcoin verify that evidence. ProofLine turns verified events into persistent credit state that independent applications can consume.

Live on Ethereum Sepolia & Creditcoin CC3 · 118 tests · every material claim reproducible from the repo
02
The problem

Economic behaviour is provable.
Creditworthiness is not portable.

A business earns on Ethereum. That history is public, permanent and cryptographically real - but it is not portable as independently verifiable credit state on another chain.

Bridging it means trusting an operator. Re-stating it means trusting a database. Both replace the one property that made the history worth having.

The question is not how to copy the history. It is how to make the credit decision depend on proof.

Remove the proof layer from ProofLine and the credit file cannot change at all
03
The insight

Attestcoin proves inclusion.
It does not prove truth.

A proof establishes that a log was included in a transaction. ProofLine then verifies successful execution, event semantics and source authorisation. None of that establishes whether the log's claim is true.

So the source contract has to make a false claim unreachable. InvoicePaid cannot exist unless the measured ERC-20 balance delta equals the invoice amount. InvoiceLate cannot exist before the due date.

Attestcoin then proves the contract reached a state its own invariants made impossible to reach falsely.

Six verification gates
1  replay      proof-derived identity
2  verifyAndEmit          precompile
3  transaction type
4  receiptStatus == 1     protocol MUST
5  matching logs found
6  emitter authorised
Gate 6 must come last: the emitter address does not exist until the receipt is decoded, which requires the proof to have verified.
Our original replay key was caller-supplied and spoofable · found by reading ASCBase.sol, fixed, recorded

The distinction

Why this is infrastructure,
not another lending app

Reusable
2
Independent consumers over one credit file. Treasury lends; CreditAccess waives security deposits. Neither imports the other, neither computes a tier, neither can write.
Load-bearing
1
One writer. Delete Attestcoin and the credit file is immutable - not degraded, not stale. The financial state transition becomes impossible.
Stateful
10
Credit is persistent state, not a one-shot reaction to a proof. History compounds; delinquency tightens terms immediately; a proven default freezes permanently.

One proven settlement moved Treasury from 70%/14% to 80%/12% and waived CreditAccess's 40% deposit - through a credit file that imports neither application. The invoice contract is our source of evidence, not the product.

08
Evidence & limitations
Measured, not assumed
  • 7.96-8.7 min end-to-end attestation
  • 321,498 gas mean production ingest, 8 samples
  • 244-669 ms proof construction once attested
  • Permissionless relay works - the worker is convenience, not trust
Found, fixed, recorded
  • Replay key was caller-spoofable
  • Accounting silently aliased its input
  • A gate-ordering test passed while proving nothing
  • Unchecked payer allowed manufactured counterparties
  • One of our claims was an inference - disproved, corrected in place
Stated plainly
  • Sybil resistance remains a production limitation. Current controls reduce self-dealing but do not establish independent counterparty identity.
  • Repayment is unsecured. Writability is in audit, so enforcement is credit-state based rather than collateral seizure. Deliberate, and Creditcoin's thesis.
  • Tier thresholds are our policy, not a Creditcoin standard. The reusable part is the shape: verified history → deterministic function → terms.
Why Creditcoin wants this

CreditFile gives Creditcoin applications a common, verifiable credit primitive instead of every application building its own cross-chain evidence pipeline.

verified economic activity → richer CreditFile
        → more applications → more Creditcoin demand
Every new source of verified history strengthens the same credit layer. Every new application can consume it.
